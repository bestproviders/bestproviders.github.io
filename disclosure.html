Affiliate disclosure & legality notice

Last updated: 2026-07-22

Affiliate disclosure

BestProviders may participate in affiliate programs. When you click certain links on this site and subscribe to a service, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. This is how the site sustains independent testing.

Affiliate relationships never influence our scores, rankings, or the pending/reviewed status shown on a provider's page. A provider we have a commercial relationship with is tested against the exact same five criteria, described in full on our methodology page, as one we don't.

Where a link on this site leads to a paid product, we mark it clearly and use rel="nofollow" attributes on outbound provider links in line with search engine guidance on sponsored/affiliate content.

Legality notice

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) is a delivery technology, not a content source. The same technology underlies many fully licensed, legal streaming services. Whether a specific IPTV service is operating legally in your region depends entirely on whether it holds proper licensing and distribution rights for the content it streams — something that varies by provider, by channel, and by your country's laws, and that we do not independently audit as part of our scoring.

We recommend readers do their own diligence on content licensing before subscribing to any IPTV service, and consult local regulations where uncertain. Nothing on this site should be taken as legal advice.

Editorial independence

Reviews on this site reflect our own hands-on testing. We do not accept payment in exchange for a specific score, and we do not publish sponsored reviews disguised as independent ones.

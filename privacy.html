Privacy policy & terms of use

Last updated: 2026-07-22

What we collect

BestProviders is a static site with no account system, comment system, or server-side backend. We do not collect names, emails, or account data because there is nowhere on this site to submit them. Standard web server logs (such as those kept by our host) may include IP address, browser type, and pages requested, consistent with typical static web hosting.

Cookies & third parties

This site does not set its own tracking cookies. Outbound links to IPTV providers are third-party sites with their own privacy policies and, in some cases, affiliate tracking parameters — we encourage you to review the privacy policy of any provider before subscribing. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log requests per their own policy.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data directly, there is no BestProviders account data to access, export, or delete. If you have privacy questions about this site specifically, you can reach out via the contact details listed in our disclosure page.

Terms of use

Reviews reflect our own testing and judgment at the time of publication and may not reflect a provider's current state if it has changed since our last update — check the "Last updated" date on any review. We are not responsible for the content, legality, or availability of third-party IPTV services we review or link to.

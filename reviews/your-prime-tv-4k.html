What Is Your Prime TV 4K?

Your Prime TV 4K is a general-entertainment IPTV subscription service sold through yourprimetv4k.com, aimed at cord-cutters who want a large live-channel bundle and VOD library rather than a niche sports- or region-specific package. Worth knowing up front: the pricing tiers, channel/VOD counts, device list, and support-hours claim on this page are, word for word and dollar for dollar, identical to dozens of other similarly-run IPTV storefronts we track. That's a genuinely useful data point for judging whether you're looking at an independent operator or one of many storefronts sharing a reseller backend. We'd rather flag that pattern directly than write a page that quietly implies this brand was built independently when the evidence in front of us says otherwise.

Is It Worth the Price?

No trial is mentioned for Your Prime TV 4K (see the FAQ below), so there's no free way to sample the service before paying for at least the shortest tier. A refund policy is referenced with a link, but its actual terms aren't published on the page we reviewed.

Streaming Quality & Reliability

The page states the standard bandwidth guidance for this category: 30 Mbps minimum for HD/FHD, 50 Mbps for 4K. No performance benchmarks, uptime figures, or buffering data are published for Your Prime TV 4K, and we have not tested streams ourselves. The single most useful thing you can do that we can't do for you yet is watch during your actual peak hours in the first days of a subscription.

Channel Lineup & Content

Live TV Categories

The page claims roughly 25,000 live channels for Your Prime TV 4K, matching the cluster's standard figure almost exactly. There's no genre or region breakdown published, so depth in any specific category (sports, news, international) is unverified.

VOD Library

"100,000+ movies and shows" is the advertised VOD figure for Your Prime TV 4K. What matters for most subscribers is a much smaller list of specific titles, and no marketing page tells you whether those are reliably available or how often the catalog refreshes.

Setup & Device Compatibility

Your Prime TV 4K lists compatibility with Smart TVs, Android boxes, Firestick, iOS, and MAG-style set-top boxes — a broad, standard device list for this category. We have not personally set this up on any device. See our device setup guide before you subscribe to any provider.

Customer Support

"24/7 Expert Support" via email and live chat is claimed for Your Prime TV 4K, without a specific contact address published on the page. We have not tested response times.

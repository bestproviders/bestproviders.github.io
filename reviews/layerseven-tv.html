Getting In: Apps, Billing, Support

Players named: IBO Player, IPTV Smarters, MAG, TiviMate and VLC. Delivery is credential-based rather than a bespoke app, which means half your playback quality is really your player’s. See the setup guide.

Support: email and whatsapp. We have not opened a ticket, so treat responsiveness as unknown until you test it yourself.

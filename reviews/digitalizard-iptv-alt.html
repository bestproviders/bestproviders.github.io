What Is DigitaLizard IPTV?

This is the fuller of two DigitaLizard IPTV listings we track — the same brand name appears on two different domains, digitalizardiptv.org and this one, digitalizardiptvi.com. We were able to retrieve this domain's public pricing page; the .org domain blocked our request, so we treat this page as the primary, fuller profile. the pricing tiers, channel/VOD counts, device list, and support-hours claim on this page are, word for word and dollar for dollar, identical to several other similarly-run IPTV storefronts we track. That's a genuinely useful data point for judging whether you're looking at an independent operator or one of several storefronts sharing a reseller backend. We're noting that plainly here rather than presenting this brand's pricing as if it were independently arrived at.

Is It Worth the Price?

No trial is mentioned (see the FAQ below). Refund policy is referenced with a link but its terms weren't included in the content we retrieved. Streaming Quality & Reliability

The page states 4K and Full HD quality with a claimed 99% server stability figure and the familiar 30/50 Mbps bandwidth guidance for HD/FHD versus 4K. As with every other stability claim in this pilot batch, there's no published methodology or third-party source behind that number, so we report it as stated, not confirmed. We have not tested streams ourselves. Bandwidth minimums are printed requirements, not a promise of consistent delivery during peak hours.

Channel Lineup & Content

Live TV Categories

Advertised totals are "25,000+ Live Channels", matching the cluster standard. No category breakdown is published, and we haven't tested the actual lineup. As with its cluster-mates, we'd caution against reading a large round number as a quality signal on its own.

VOD Library

"100,000+ movies and shows" is claimed for VOD. What matters for most subscribers is a much smaller list of specific titles, and no marketing page tells you whether those specific ones are reliably included, or how often the lineup actually gets updated.

Setup & Device Compatibility

Device support is listed as Smart TVs, Android devices, iOS, Firestick, MAG devices, and Android boxes. We have not tested setup on any of these ourselves. Notably, this page's legal disclaimer states the service provides access to "content that is already available on the web" and does not host content directly — worth reading in full before subscribing. See our device setup guide for general setup guidance.

Customer Support

"24/7 Support" via email and live chat is advertised, without a specific contact address published. We have not tested response times ourselves, and given the identical support language used across several cluster-mates, we wouldn't assume brand-specific responsiveness until we'd tested it directly.

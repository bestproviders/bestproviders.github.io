Verdict

On public information, Boss IPTV Store is not meaningfully distinguishable from the 24 storefronts sharing its price card and its copy. That is not an accusation — there is no evidence it is worse than them either. Buy short, pay by card, judge it on support response and peak-hour stability, which are the only things that genuinely vary here.

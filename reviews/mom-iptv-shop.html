What Is Mom IPTV Shop?

Mom IPTV Shop is a general IPTV subscription storefront at momiptvshop.com. Before anything else, a disclosure that applies to a large share of the providers we track: the pricing tiers, channel/VOD counts, device list, and support-hours claim on this page are, word for word and dollar for dollar, identical to dozens of other similarly-run IPTV storefronts we track. That's a genuinely useful data point for judging whether you're looking at an independent operator or one of many storefronts sharing a reseller backend. We'd rather flag that pattern directly than write a page that quietly implies this brand was built independently when the evidence in front of us says otherwise.

Streaming Quality & Reliability

Mom IPTV Shop's marketing page states 4K and Full HD quality and recommends a minimum of 30 Mbps for HD/FHD streams and at least 50 Mbps for 4K — printed bandwidth requirements, not performance claims, and we report them as such. We have not tested Mom IPTV Shop's streams ourselves, so we're not describing buffering behavior or live-sports performance as if we had.

Channel Lineup & Content

Live TV Categories

Mom IPTV Shop states over 25,000 live channels, in line with the shared cluster's standard claim. We can't verify what portion is duplicated across regions or how current the EPG data actually is.

VOD Library

Mom IPTV Shop claims "100,000+ VOD" titles. We report that figure because it's what's printed on the page, not because we've counted titles ourselves. VOD libraries this large in this market are often padded with older or lower-demand titles.

Setup & Device Compatibility

Mom IPTV Shop's listed device compatibility (Smart TVs, Android, iOS, Firestick, MAG boxes) is standard for this category and stated plainly on the page. We have not confirmed install friction or app stability ourselves. See our device setup guide for general setup guidance across any provider.

Customer Support

Mom IPTV Shop advertises "24/7" support reachable by email and live chat. We have not filed a support request ourselves, so we can't tell you how long a real response actually takes — treat the always-on claim as marketing copy until you've tested it.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • The long-term discount tier offers a genuinely low effective monthly cost if that commitment suits you
  • Bandwidth requirements are stated clearly so you can sanity-check your own connection before buying
  • 24/7 support is offered through two channels (email and live chat), at least on paper
  • Activation is described as fast, with credentials emailed promptly after purchase
  • Broad advertised device list (Smart TVs, Android boxes, Firestick, iOS, MAG boxes) covers most common home setups

Cons

  • No free trial is advertised, so there's no low-risk way to sample the service before paying
  • No independent streaming-quality or reliability testing exists yet for this specific brand
  • Pricing, channel/VOD counts, and marketing copy are effectively identical to dozens of other tracked storefronts, making brand-specific differentiation hard to establish
  • No category breakdown for the advertised channel count, so genre depth (sports, international) is unverified
  • Standard plans include only one simultaneous connection; multi-device households need an unpriced add-on

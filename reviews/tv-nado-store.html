Getting In: Apps, Billing, Support

Players named: IPTV Smarters and MAG. Delivery is credential-based rather than a bespoke app, which means half your playback quality is really your player’s. See the setup guide.

Payment: Stripe. A card route means a chargeback stays possible.

Support: email, live chat and whatsapp. We have not opened a ticket, so treat responsiveness as unknown until you test it yourself.

Our Read on TV Nado Store

On public information, TV Nado Store is not meaningfully distinguishable from the 24 storefronts sharing its price card and its copy. That is not an accusation — there is no evidence it is worse than them either.

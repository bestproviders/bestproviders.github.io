What Is One Dollar IPTV Store?

One Dollar IPTV Store's domain promises budget pricing right in the name, but the pricing page we pulled doesn't actually show a one-dollar tier anywhere — its cheapest listed plan is $14.95 for one month, in line with the same four-tier structure we've found across several other storefronts: the pricing tiers, channel/VOD counts, device list, and support-hours claim on this page are, word for word and dollar for dollar, identical to several other similarly-run IPTV storefronts we track. That's a genuinely useful data point for judging whether you're looking at an independent operator or one of several storefronts sharing a reseller backend. We flag the name-versus-pricing gap not to accuse anyone of anything, only because a shopper clicking through expecting a literal dollar-a-month plan should know that's not what's listed today.

Is It Worth the Price?

No free trial is mentioned (see the FAQ below for the $1-plan question specifically). A refund policy page is linked but its actual terms weren't included in the content we could retrieve — treat that as unconfirmed rather than assume any specific policy. Judged on its real $14.95-and-up pricing, it's not meaningfully cheaper than the general market rate for this category.

Streaming Quality & Reliability

The site states 4K and HD quality with a claimed "99% server stability" figure — the only provider in this pilot batch to publish an uptime-style number at all. We can't verify that figure independently; it's not accompanied by a methodology, timeframe, or third-party monitoring source on the page, so we're reporting it as a stated claim, not a confirmed fact. Bandwidth guidance matches the cluster standard: 30 Mbps for HD/FHD, 50 Mbps for 4K Ultra HD. We have not tested actual stream performance ourselves.

Channel Lineup & Content

Live TV Categories

Advertised content is "over 25,000 live TV channels", matching the cluster's standard figures almost word for word. No category breakdown is published, so we can't independently confirm depth in any specific genre like live sports or regional international channels.

VOD Library

"Upwards of 100,000 movies and shows" is claimed for VOD. As with its cluster-mates, we'd treat a round, large advertised total as a starting point rather than a quality signal on its own — the number that actually matters is whether the handful of titles you personally want are included.

Setup & Device Compatibility

Device support is listed as Smart TVs, Android, iOS, Firestick, MAG devices, and Android boxes — the standard list for this category. We have not installed or tested this on any device ourselves. See our device setup guide for what to check on your specific hardware before subscribing to any provider.

Customer Support

Support is listed with more specificity than most cluster-mates: an email address and a WhatsApp contact link, plus a "24/7" availability claim. We have not contacted support ourselves to confirm real response times, but having a named channel (WhatsApp) rather than just a generic "live chat" reference is a small, verifiable point in its favor over some peers.

Verdict — Should You Subscribe?

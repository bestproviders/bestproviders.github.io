What Is Flixtro Store?

Flixtro Store sells IPTV access through flixtrostore.com, positioned as a general-entertainment package rather than a specialty one. the pricing tiers, channel/VOD counts, device list, and support-hours claim on this page are, word for word and dollar for dollar, identical to dozens of other similarly-run IPTV storefronts we track. That's a genuinely useful data point for judging whether you're looking at an independent operator or one of many storefronts sharing a reseller backend. We'd rather flag that pattern directly than write a page that quietly implies this brand was built independently when the evidence in front of us says otherwise. We're not going to pretend this brand was built independently from scratch when the evidence says otherwise.

Streaming Quality & Reliability

We have not tested Flixtro Store's streams. The page's quality claims (4K, Full HD) are standard marketing language for this category and shouldn't be read as a tested result. If reliability under load matters to you, test it yourself in the first days of a short-term plan, ideally during the exact time slot you'd normally watch, since that's when shared infrastructure is most likely to show strain.

Channel Lineup & Content

Live TV Categories

"25,000+ Live Channels" is the advertised figure for Flixtro Store. A number this large, in this market, commonly includes heavy duplication across regional feeds; that's a general pattern worth knowing, not a claim specific to this provider, since we haven't opened the service to check.

VOD Library

Flixtro Store's VOD library is claimed at over 100,000 titles, again a round number without a genre breakdown. We'd treat that as a starting point, not a quality signal on its own.

Setup & Device Compatibility

Flixtro Store supports the usual device roster for this market. We haven't personally installed or configured it. If you're new to IPTV setup generally, our device setup guide is a good starting point regardless of provider.

Customer Support

"24/7 Expert Support" via email and live chat is claimed for Flixtro Store, without a specific contact address published on the page. We have not tested response times.

Verify This Before You Subscribe

Before paying for more than the shortest plan, we'd check three things ourselves: whether the live-chat or email support channel actually responds to a real pre-sale question within a reasonable window, since "24/7" is a claim until tested; whether the refund policy page actually spells out a window and conditions once you read it in full, since the pricing page references one without stating terms; and whether the service holds up during your actual peak-viewing hours, since that's when a reseller-style service under shared load is most likely to show its real reliability.

Verdict — Should You Subscribe?

Flixtro Store offers nothing in its public marketing that separates it from its cluster-mates, and we haven't tested it to find a difference that isn't visible on the page. Treat this profile as a starting point for your own research, not a recommendation either way. We'll update this page in place the moment that changes.

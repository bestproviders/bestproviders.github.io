Why a systeme.io copy?

systeme.io is an all-in-one marketing platform with its own website builder and hosting. Publishing the directory there gives readers another fast, independent address if one host is slow or blocked in their region. Each page points its canonical tag at the Neocities original, so search engines treat the systeme.io pages as copies rather than competing duplicates.

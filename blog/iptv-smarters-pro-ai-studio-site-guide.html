Frequently asked questions

Is iptv-smarters-pro.ai.studio the official IPTV Smarters Pro app developer?

It presents itself as a setup guide and subscription storefront built around the IPTV Smarters Pro player. The player app and the channel subscription are separate things: the app plays content, and a provider supplies it. Check our IPTV Smarters Pro review for what we could and could not verify.

How many pages does the site have?

Its sitemap lists 59 URLs, including 36 guide pages, 6 provider-directory pages and 9 translated home pages.

Where do I start if Smarters Pro keeps buffering?

Use the "Keeps Buffering: 7 Fixes" guide listed above, then the cache-clearing guide. If buffering continues on every channel, the problem is usually the provider’s servers or your connection rather than the app.

Is IPTV Smarters Pro free?

The player app is generally free to download; the channels come from a separate paid subscription. The site has a dedicated page explaining the difference.
